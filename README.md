# 📖 Bible & Songbook

An offline-focused Android **KJV Bible and Lutheran hymn/songbook** application with integrated Bible study tools and optional downloadable resources.

Bible & Songbook is now available in **two editions**:

- 📱 **Native Android Edition** — new native rebuild with expanded reading and study features
- 🌐 **WebView Edition** — original established version of Bible & Songbook

The Bible, songbook, notes, bookmarks, highlights, favorites, reading progress, and installed study resources are designed to work without an active internet connection.

> [!CAUTION]
> ## 🧪 Native Edition is currently in testing
> The Native Android Edition is a major rebuild and is still undergoing real-world testing.
>
> **Bugs, crashes, UI issues, and features that do not behave as expected may still occur.**
>
> If you encounter a problem, please report it through GitHub. The established **WebView Edition** remains available for users who prefer the original application while the Native Edition continues to mature.

---

## 📥 Download and Install

The latest Android APKs are available from the repository's **Releases** page.

Each release provides two editions:

### 📱 Native Android Edition — Recommended / Testing

The new native rebuild and the primary edition planned for future development.

Download the APK labeled:

`NATIVE`

### 🌐 WebView Edition — Original / Established

The original HTML/WebView-based Bible & Songbook application.

Download the APK labeled:

`WEBVIEW`

Open the desired APK on your Android device and follow the installation prompt.

Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager because the app is distributed directly rather than through Google Play.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The Native and WebView editions currently use the same Android application ID.
>
> They are alternative versions of Bible & Songbook and **cannot normally be installed side-by-side**.

# 📱 Native Android Edition

The Native Edition is a major rebuild of Bible & Songbook using native Android components rather than the original WebView interface.

The goal isn't simply to reproduce the old interface. The native architecture provides a foundation for a more capable offline Bible reader and study application.

### Highlights

- Fully native Android Bible reader
- Bundled KJV Bible
- Red-letter Words of Christ
- Built-in Songbook
- Modern verse-selection tools
- Highlights and colored underlines
- Notes, bookmarks, and favorites
- Explain and Compare tools
- Dictionaries, lexicons, and original-language study
- Cross references
- SWORD Bible and study-resource support
- Reading progress and plans
- E-reader-style text customization
- Native Android text-to-speech
- Light, Dark, and AMOLED themes
- Backup and restore

The **Native Edition is the primary version planned for continued development and new features.**

---

# 🌐 WebView Edition

The WebView Edition is the original Bible & Songbook application.

It retains the established HTML/WebView-based reader and interface for users who prefer the original experience or encounter issues while testing the Native Edition.

The WebView Edition will remain available alongside the Native Edition while development of the native rebuild continues.

# 📖 Bible Reader

The Native Edition includes a complete **King James Version (KJV)** Bible designed for offline use.

### Reading

- Complete KJV Bible
- Offline Bible reading
- Navigation by book, chapter, and reference
- Swipe between chapters
- Previous and next chapter controls
- Resume your last reading position
- Remember your position within a chapter
- Reading progress tracking
- Reading plans and study guides
- Optional red-letter Words of Christ
- Native Android text-to-speech with word following

### Verse Actions

Tap a verse to select it.

Additional verses can then be tapped to create a multi-verse selection.

Selected Scripture supports:

- Highlight
- Colored underline
- Copy
- Explain
- Note
- Compare
- Save
- Share

Long-press text when you want Android's normal text selection for a specific word or phrase.

---

# 🎨 Reader Customization

The Native Edition includes an e-reader-style formatting interface accessible through the **Aa** button while reading.

Reader options include:

- Font selection
- Text size
- Line spacing
- Verse spacing
- Reader margins
- Verse, Continuous, and Reading layouts
- Verse-number controls
- Text alignment
- Reading indentation
- Chapter headings
- Paper and Sepia reading backgrounds
- Night and Black reading backgrounds
- Light, Dark, and AMOLED application themes
- Reader presets for quickly switching between different reading styles

---

# 💡 Explain

**Explain** is designed for understanding an entire verse or passage.

Select one or more verses and choose **Explain**.

Explain can use installed passage-level resources including:

- Bible commentaries
- Cross references
- Study notes and related resources

Additional commentary resources can be installed through the integrated SWORD Resource Library.

Explain is intentionally separate from **Study**.

---

# 🔤 Study

**Study** is designed primarily for investigating individual words and terms.

Long-press or select text in the Bible and choose **Study**.

Available study information can include:

### Definitions

- English word definitions
- Bible dictionary entries
- Biblical people
- Biblical places
- Biblical subjects and terms

### Strong's & Original Languages

- Strong's numbers
- Hebrew and Greek words
- Lemmas
- Transliteration
- Pronunciation information
- Lexical definitions
- KJV usage information
- Hebrew and Greek morphology
- Original-language verse analysis

The Study interface separates definitions and morphology/original-language information into dedicated views.

---

# ↔️ Compare

Select one or more verses and choose **Compare** to view the passage across multiple installed Bible translations.

The bundled KJV is immediately available.

Additional Bible translations can be added through the SWORD Resource Library.

You only need to install the translations you actually want rather than downloading an entire Bible library.

---

# 🔗 Cross References

The Native Edition includes an offline cross-reference system using **CrossReferences.org** data.

Cross References can display:

- Direct verse connections
- Incoming connections
- Multi-level connections
- Connection strength
- Related Scripture
- Connected phrases
- Connections across all 66 Bible books

Once the required data is prepared by the app, these connections can be explored offline.

---

# 📚 SWORD Resource Library

The Native Edition integrates support for **SWORD** Bible study resources.

Optional resources include:

### Bible Translations

Install additional supported Bible translations for offline reading and Compare.

### Commentaries

Install passage-level commentary resources for Explain and deeper Bible study.

### Dictionaries & Lexicons

Install additional dictionaries and lexical resources for word study.

### Study Books

Additional compatible books and study resources can also be installed.

The resource library displays download, installation, and indexing progress.

Resources remain available offline after installation.

---

# ⚙️ Automatic Setup

The Native Edition automatically prepares the core resources needed by the application.

**You do not need to manually visit Downloads & Library before using the app.**

After installation, you can begin reading and using the core study functionality normally.

The Downloads & Library section is primarily for adding **optional resources**, such as:

- Additional Bible translations
- Commentaries
- Dictionaries
- Lexicons
- Study books

This keeps the base application useful immediately while allowing users to build a larger offline study library if desired.

---

# 🔴 Red-Letter Bible

The Native Edition includes word/phrase-level red-letter data identifying the Words of Christ.

Unlike systems that simply color an entire verse red, Bible & Songbook can identify the relevant words or phrases within the verse.

Red-letter display can be enabled or disabled from the reader settings.

---

# 🎵 Songbook

Bible & Songbook includes a complete offline hymn/songbook.

Features include:

- 673 hymns
- Search by title, number, or words
- Song categories
- Favorite songs
- Swipe between songs
- Offline access

The song collection is organized and numbered to correspond with *Hymns and Songs of Zion*, the hymnal used in Apostolic Lutheran congregations.

This allows the app to be used alongside the physical hymnal while keeping familiar hymn numbers and organization.

> Bible & Songbook is an independent project and is not an official application of, sponsored by, or affiliated with the Apostolic Lutheran Church of America or its Book Concern.

# 🔒 Privacy

Bible & Songbook is designed as an **offline-focused application**.

The following information is stored locally on the device:

- Bible and songbook content
- Favorites
- Bookmarks
- Highlights
- Underlines
- Notes
- Reading progress
- Reader settings
- Installed study resources

**The app does not include advertising or analytics.**

## 🌐 Internet Access

The Android application requests the `INTERNET` permission for functions that require retrieving optional resources.

Internet access may be used for user-initiated functions such as:

- Downloading additional Bible translations
- Downloading commentaries
- Downloading dictionaries and lexicons
- Downloading study books
- Retrieving or updating supported resource catalogs
- Re-downloading supported resources

Normal Bible reading, songbook use, notes, highlights, bookmarks, favorites, reading progress, and already-installed study resources do not require an active internet connection.

External web searches and source/attribution links are handed to the device's external browser.

# 💾 Local Data & Updates

Favorites, bookmarks, highlights, underlines, notes, reading progress, installed study resources, and application settings are stored locally on the device.

Uninstalling the application may remove locally stored application data.

Updates signed with the same application signing key can normally be installed over an existing version without removing local data.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Always make a backup of important notes and other personal data before installing testing builds or making major version changes.

# 🚧 Native Edition Testing

The Native Edition is under active development.

Although it has undergone testing, this is the first public generation of the native rebuild and users should expect that some issues remain.

Possible issues may include:

- UI/layout problems on certain devices
- Resource download or installation problems
- Unexpected behavior with particular SWORD modules
- Bugs in newer study tools
- Device-specific Android behavior
- Crashes or other unfinished behavior

If you find a problem, please open a **GitHub Issue** with as much information as possible.

Useful information includes:

- Phone/tablet model
- Android version
- Bible & Songbook version
- Native or WebView Edition
- What you were doing when the problem occurred
- Screenshots when applicable
- Steps that reproduce the issue

Bug reports and feature suggestions are welcome.
